Home energy that actually works.

42W coordinates your solar, batteries, grid, and EV charger in real time — so your house runs on its own power. One Go binary. Runs on a Raspberry Pi. No cloud required.

View on GitHub → Join the Discord

Local-first

Control loop runs on-device. Grid frequency balances every second — cloud APIs can't keep up.

MPC planner

48-hour horizon. Electricity prices + weather forecasts decide when to charge, discharge, or hold.

Digital twins

Online models learn your roof, your load pattern, and fill in missing price data.

19 drivers

Sungrow, Ferroamp, Huawei, Deye, Solis, SMA, Fronius, Kostal, Pixii, Easee and more.

EV-aware dispatch

Batteries stop discharging when your car is charging. No energy round-trips.

Home Assistant

Built-in MQTT autodiscovery. Sensors and controls appear automatically.
